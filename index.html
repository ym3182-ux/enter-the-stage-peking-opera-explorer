ARCHIVE CARD / 01

Jing / 净
The painted-face role

Jing, also called hualian (花脸, “painted face”), is a broad performance category—not one single personality.

LOOK FOR

More than colour

Read the face pattern together with costume, voice, and movement. One colour is a clue—not a complete translation.

ON STAGE

Designed for distance

Bold makeup enlarges expression so that a character can be read from far across a theatre.

REMEMBER

A trained role system

Different Jing specialities emphasise singing, acting, or martial movement. Makeup is only one part of the craft.

General Yao Qi

An older general and father. When his son causes a grave mistake, Yao Qi has to carry both authority and worry onto the stage.

JING EXAMPLE / 净

Bao Zheng

The Case of Executing Chen Shimei / 鍘美案

A famous judge known for fairness. His story asks a simple but difficult question: can justice hold when power tries to protect itself?

What to notice: a role category is read through the whole performance, not through face colour alone.
